RUDRAVAAX - NOT JUST A NAME.

Before the name, there was a voice. Before the sound, there was silence. And before the world could hear Rudravaax, there was a young boy trying to understand what the music inside him was asking for.

2018 — Present A story in sound
01

Every sound has a beginning

Before the first track

Some stories begin with an opportunity. Others begin with a feeling.

This one began with a phone, a voice, and a curiosity that refused to disappear. There was no grand studio waiting in the background. No expensive equipment. No perfect conditions. Only a young mind discovering that music could become more than something to listen to.

It could become something to create. Something to question. Something to build an identity around — even when nobody else could see what that identity might become.

This is not a story about becoming successful overnight. It is about the years nobody saw, the experiments that did not work, the sound that kept changing, and the person who kept returning to it.

The timeline

Every era left a mark.

Different years. Different sounds. Different versions of the same dream. One continuous journey towards finding a voice that feels unmistakably its own.

01 2018

Origin

The first spark

In 2018, a young boy began singing through his father's phone. His world was small, but the possibilities inside his head were not. There was no professional microphone, no studio, and no carefully designed plan. There was simply a voice looking for a place to exist.

StarMaker became one of the first places where that voice could be heard. Song after song, he began discovering the strange connection between emotion, melody, and expression. He did not yet understand the mechanics of music. He only knew that something about it felt right.

He did not begin with a career in mind. He began because he wanted to sing.

02 2020–2021

Echoes

From singing to creating

By 2020, the early phase of singing had given him something more important than polished recordings: the desire to go further. The songs were imperfect. The recordings were limited. But each attempt left behind a lesson.

Gradually, listening turned into curiosity. Curiosity turned into experimentation. Existing lyrics found their way onto beats he made himself. He began looking beyond the vocal and towards the entire sound surrounding it.

In 2021, another shift began. He started writing his own lyrics and took composition more seriously around December. Music was no longer just a place to perform. It was becoming a place to put his own thoughts.

The voice wanted more than a melody. It wanted something of its own to say.

03 The early years

Silence

The struggle nobody heard

Not everyone understood what music meant to him. When his father discovered a song on the phone and played it to the family, the reaction was not the encouragement he had hoped for. There was criticism, scolding, and punishment — because the people around him did not see the same future he was beginning to imagine.

For a young creator, that kind of moment can make a dream feel difficult to explain. Something deeply personal suddenly becomes something to defend. The passion remains, but the confidence around it has to survive questions it cannot yet answer.

He was still learning. Still making mistakes. Still trying to understand why a sound felt right in his head but wrong when he heard it back. The work was far from perfect, and the journey offered no guarantees.

Some of the most important work happens before anybody believes it is worth hearing.

04 May–June 2022

Collision

Two creative directions

In May 2022, an Instagram connection introduced him to Aman Kumar, known as A₹chak. What began through social media developed into a creative partnership.

Their strengths leaned in different directions. Rudravaax was increasingly interested in production, inspired by the energy and sound of artists such as Yo Yo Honey Singh. A₹chak brought a strong interest in writing. Different instincts gave their collaboration a new direction.

In June 2022, they created tracks including Classy Launde and Kisi Se Kiss. The songs were made with the tools they had: phones, limited resources, and an ambition much larger than the technical quality they could achieve at the time.

Those early collaborations may no longer be available online, but their importance was never limited to where the tracks ended up. They were part of the process through which an artist learned to work with another creative mind.

Sometimes, a new direction begins when two different ideas meet.

05 2023

Frequency

Learning the language of sound

By 2023, production had become a more serious part of the journey. Making a beat was only the beginning. The next challenge was understanding why some sounds worked together while others fought for space.

BandLab became part of the process from around April through October. Timing, texture, balance, mixing, and mastering were no longer distant technical terms. They were problems to solve, one project at a time.

The learning was practical and often imperfect. A mix could sound different through different earphones. A beat could feel powerful in one moment and crowded in the next. Every experiment revealed another detail he had not noticed before.

He was not only making music anymore. He was learning how to listen.

06 2024

Alchemy

Turning experiments into experience

In 2024, attention shifted further towards mixing and mastering. The focus was no longer simply on completing songs, but on understanding how a collection of individual sounds could become one coherent experience.

Projects accumulated. Ideas multiplied. By the end of the year, the catalogue contained more than 200 songs and projects across different stages of development. Not every experiment needed to become a release. Each one became part of a growing education in sound.

This was the unglamorous side of creation: repeated listening, small adjustments, lessons from mistakes, and the patience to return to a track that was not yet saying what he wanted it to say.

The craft was changing. So was the person behind it.

07 2025

Exodus

Leaving room for a new beginning

By 2025, years of experimentation had created a large body of work. But a catalogue full of songs did not necessarily represent the artist he wanted to become.

He made the decision to take down previous releases from music platforms and prepare for a fresh start. It was a change in direction: a willingness to leave an earlier public identity behind rather than let it define everything that came next.

Starting again does not erase the past. The lessons remain, even when the old work is no longer the centre of the story. Every rough recording, unfinished idea, and previous release had helped shape his understanding of what he wanted to create.

The past was not wasted. It had prepared the ground.

08 2026

Rebirth

A name. A direction. A new chapter.

In 2026, the journey entered a new phase. The earlier identity, Jaadugar, belonged to a previous chapter. A new artistic identity emerged: Rudravaax.

The name represents a renewed direction, not the claim that the learning is over. He continues to develop as a vocalist, producer, composer, lyricist, and engineer. He continues to explore how the elements of his music can work together to create something that feels personal and distinct.

The ambition reaches beyond a single release. Rudravaax is building a wider creative identity — one that brings together music, visual presentation, independent work, and a closer connection with the people who choose to listen.

A new name does not change where the journey began. It changes how the next chapter is written.

09 The road ahead

Pursuit

Building beyond the present

The next stage is not defined by one guaranteed outcome. It is defined by the work still waiting to be done: stronger records, sharper production, a clearer identity, and a growing relationship with an audience that connects with the music.

The vision is to create music that carries energy and personality — bringing together hip-hop, rap, electronic influences, and melodies that belong to his own creative world. Every release is an opportunity to refine that direction.

There will be more to learn, more decisions to make, and more work that never reaches the public. Progress will not always look dramatic from the outside. Sometimes it will be one better mix, one stronger idea, or one song that finally sounds the way it was imagined.

The goal is not to pretend the journey is complete. The goal is to keep moving.

10 Ongoing

Legacy

Still being written

Legacy is a word that belongs to the future, but its foundations are laid in the present. It is built through choices, consistency, the courage to improve, and the ability to keep creating when the response is uncertain.

Rudravaax is still on that road. The story has no final scene yet. There are sounds still to discover, projects still to shape, and experiences that have not happened.

The years behind him explain how the journey began. They do not decide how far it can go. That part will be written through the music, the work, and the connection built with listeners over time.

This chapter is not a conclusion. It is a promise to continue creating.

Between the past and the future

The world may hear the music.
Only the journey knows the cost.

Every finished track carries a history that listeners may never see: the first idea, the imperfect recording, the hours spent learning, the versions left behind, and the decision to try again. The final sound is only the part that makes it out of the room.

The person behind the name

Rudravaax. Still becoming.

Behind the artist name is Nishant Malik, an independent creator from Kurmali, in the Shamli district of Uttar Pradesh, India. His journey began in childhood, long before the tools, techniques, and plans became clearer.

He learned by doing: singing, writing, producing, listening back, and trying to understand what could be better. The process remains ongoing. Experience has shaped his direction, but curiosity continues to shape his work.

The name is new. The journey behind it has been years in the making.

The story continues

THIS IS NOT THE END.

There are still songs to make, lessons to learn, and chapters that have not found their titles. The future is not a finished script. It is a space to create, to grow, and to discover what the music can become.

From a phone in 2018 to an independent identity in 2026, the journey has changed its sound more than once. But the reason to create remains.

About Rudravaax

Rudravaax is the independent artist identity of Nishant Malik, a vocalist, music producer, composer, lyricist, mixing and mastering engineer, and graphic designer from Shamli, Uttar Pradesh, India. Beginning his musical journey in 2018, he developed his craft through years of experimentation, self-learning, and independent creation.

After earlier work under the name Jaadugar, 2026 marks a new creative chapter as Rudravaax. His ongoing direction brings together hip-hop, rap, electronic influences, vocals, and a developing visual identity. His focus remains on improving the craft, building an authentic audience connection, and creating work that reflects his evolving sound.

This biography documents a journey in progress. The next chapter will be shaped by the work still to come.
