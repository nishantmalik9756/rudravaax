BEFORE YOU SUBMIT

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I send my audio files?

Submit the request form first. Then send your audio files by email to rudravaax@gmail.com, following the instructions provided by RudraVX.

When does the 7–10 day period start?

The expected turnaround starts after the required project files have been received, not simply when you submit this request form.

Do I need to pay 40% in advance?

Yes. A 40% advance payment is mandatory before work begins. The amount for your chosen package is shown in the order summary.

Can I submit files after 7 days?

The standard submission window is 7 days from your request. Contact RudraVX if you need an extension before the window expires.
