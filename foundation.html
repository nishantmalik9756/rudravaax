ABOUT THE FOUNDATION

Built for serious artists.

What is RudraVX Foundation?

RudraVX Foundation is an artist-support program designed to identify artists with strong music knowledge and provide them with access to eligible RudraVX services at a reduced price.


Artists must apply first. Once their application is approved, they become eligible to take the Foundation qualification tests. The final result determines their Foundation benefit.

TEST RULES

The test is serious.

Read the rules carefully before beginning any Foundation test.

01

200 Questions

Every Foundation test contains 200 multiple-choice music-related questions.

02

50 Minutes

The timer begins immediately when the test starts.

03

Sequential Tests

The next test becomes available only after the current test has been successfully cleared.

04

Automatic Submission

When the 50-minute timer reaches zero, the test is automatically submitted.

05

Secure Examination

The examination interface uses supported browser monitoring such as fullscreen and visibility detection.

06

No External Assistance

Artists are expected to complete the Foundation tests using their own knowledge.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How many tests are there?

There are five Foundation tests. Each test contains 200 questions.

How much time is given for each test?

Each test has a 50-minute time limit. When the timer reaches zero, the test is automatically submitted.

Can I directly take Test 02?

No. Tests are sequential. The next test becomes available only after the previous test has been successfully cleared.

What is the maximum Foundation discount?

The maximum Foundation discount is 60%, regardless of whether the final score is above 60%, above 80%, or 100%.

Are the questions difficult?

Yes. The Foundation is designed around advanced and professional-level music knowledge rather than basic trivia.

Will every artist receive the same questions?

The examination system is designed to use a large question pool and randomized question selection so that different attempts can receive different questions.

Can I use Google to log into my Foundation account?

Foundation authentication and normal Google authentication are separate account paths. Foundation benefits require successful Foundation account authentication.

Can the Foundation discount be changed manually?

No. Eligible Foundation benefits are calculated and verified by the system.
