RudraVX Advertising

Take Your Music Further.

Promote your music, videos and projects through Google Ads, YouTube Ads and Meta Ads with campaign planning and advertising support.

Who Can Use RudraVX Advertising?

This advertising service is currently available only to artists under RudraVX Label Partnership and artists whose music has been distributed through RudraVX.

0%

Additional RudraVX Advertising Fee

RudraVX does not charge an additional percentage-based fee on your advertising spend. Your advertising budget is paid according to the selected platform and campaign setup.

Advertising Budget

There is no universal fixed maximum advertising budget across these platforms.

Google Ads officially has no minimum spending commitment, and advertisers can set their own budget. Campaign budget controls can also vary depending on the campaign type and account.

For YouTube Ads and Meta Ads, available minimums, spending controls and campaign limits may vary by platform, campaign type, account and other factors.

Your actual advertising budget will therefore be decided by you and configured according to the platform and campaign requirements.
