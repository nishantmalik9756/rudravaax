About Rudravaax

An artist's identity is more than a name. It is a sound, a vision and a journey.

RVX
INDEPENDENT MUSIC

Music without creative boundaries.

Rudravaax, also known as Nishant Malik, is an independent music artist working across rap, songwriting, music production, composition and audio engineering.

His creative direction brings together elements of Hip-Hop, Trap, Pop, Reggaeton and EDM. His approach focuses on building energetic sounds, memorable hooks and a distinct musical identity.

As a rapper, lyricist and producer, Rudravaax explores different sounds while developing his own artistic style.

RudraVX is the wider creative ecosystem built around this vision, connecting music production, mixing and mastering, distribution, collaboration and artist-focused services.

02 / TRANSPARENT ROYALTY MODEL

How the royalty structure works.

Service-wise royalty distribution

RudraVX proposes a service-based royalty model instead of charging conventional upfront production and service fees. The applicable percentage will be agreed upon before each project begins.

SERVICE ROYALTY
Music Production 10%
Audio Distribution 5%
Mixing & Mastering 5%
Selected RudraVX Collaboration 0% production royalty

Important: These are proposed service royalties, not a confirmed combined lifetime percentage. The royalty base, duration, deductions, collection method, ownership and whether different service royalties combine must be specified in the signed project agreement.

03 / PROJECT CONDITIONS

Production & Distribution Policy.

Music Produced by RudraVX

  • If an artist commissions production through RudraVX, the proposed production royalty is 10%.
  • Under the proposed service model, distribution of that project is also handled through RudraVX.
  • Distribution royalty is separately proposed at 5%.
  • Distribution requirements, platform access, ownership and termination conditions must be agreed in writing before production starts.

Independent Production

  • Artists may bring music produced independently or by another producer.
  • Artists may approach RudraVX for mixing and mastering.
  • Production-related royalties do not apply to independently produced work unless separately agreed.
  • Mixing and mastering arrangements are governed by their own project agreements.
04 / LABEL–ARTIST PARTNERSHIP

Build a long-term creative relationship.

Artist Partnership

  • Partnership is intended to be voluntary and non-exclusive unless expressly agreed otherwise.
  • Artists retain the ability to leave the partnership according to the agreed exit terms.
  • The proposed participation target is four projects within every six-month period.
  • Project definitions, counting rules, exceptions and timelines must be agreed in advance.

Partnership Exit Conditions

  • The proposed agreement mentions a $300 early-exit charge in certain circumstances.
  • This amount is not an automatic penalty and requires legal review, clear disclosure and express agreement.
  • Artists must be informed of all applicable conditions before joining.
  • Existing project rights, royalty obligations and outstanding payments must be settled according to the written agreement and applicable law.
05 / CREATIVE FREEDOM

Collaboration should be FREE.

RudraVX believes that creativity grows through collaboration. When RudraVX selects and invites an artist for an official collaboration, the production contribution is intended to be free, with no production royalty for that selected collaboration under the proposed terms. If an artist independently approaches RudraVX requesting production services for a collaboration, the normal production royalty terms may apply. All participating artists must agree on credits, ownership, revenue splits and distribution before release.

06 / ELIGIBILITY

Who can join RudraVX?

🇮🇳 Indian Artists

The proposed ecosystem is exclusively intended for eligible artists based in India, subject to identity, eligibility and service requirements.

Creative Independence

Artists can explore individual services without automatically entering a lifetime artist partnership. Separate service agreements will govern separate projects.

Important Terms & Transparency

RudraVX is a proposed artist-services ecosystem. All percentages and conditions displayed here are proposed commercial terms and must be confirmed in written agreements. Lifetime royalty calculations, combined service percentages, distribution exclusivity, master ownership, publishing rights, royalty accounting, termination and any early-exit charge require appropriate legal review before implementation. No website statement gives RudraVX the authority to impose criminal punishment or place an artist in government custody. Any dispute or alleged agreement violation must be handled through lawful procedures under applicable Indian law. Artists should carefully review the relevant agreement and obtain independent advice before accepting any terms.
